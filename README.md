## About Me

I'm a **Tech Lead** on the DevSecOps team at _MODO_, focused on integrating security into every stage of the software lifecycle. I hold an [AWS Certified Security Specialty](https://www.credly.com/badges/625e1743-4cc7-435a-980e-0f7b98da8dea) certification and have extensive experience in cloud security, automation, and infrastructure as code.

I enjoy designing secure architectures from scratch, with a strong focus on **shift-left security** and on improving **Developer Experience (DevEx)** along the way. Beyond hands-on engineering, I also collaborate on **PCI DSS certification, audits, and compliance**, working across teams to keep infrastructure and processes audit-ready.
